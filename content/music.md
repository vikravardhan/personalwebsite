# Music

I am glad music exists in this world.

Irrespective of situations, music has been my friend in all kinds of emotions. I am sure I am not the only who feels this way.

When my friends from all across the country asked me to share my music recommendations, I didn't want to suggest the usual Bollywood or International music (which I love btw.)

Instead, I wanted to introduce them to the songs meaningful to me and alien to them, yet connects us both.

I am from Hyderabad, India. Telugu music became a part of my life even before I knew what music was.

I keep telling my friends, "I wish you knew Telugu because the lyrics are too beautiful for you to miss out."

I might not help them understand the language, but surely can help them discover the best - from the most romantic to the badass BGMs.

This is an attempt at introducing good music to you.

I handpicked 900+ songs. You can save it on Spotify if you'd like.
